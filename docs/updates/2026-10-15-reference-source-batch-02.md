# Reference-source verification: second batch

Another five configured watch-reference sites were checked with exact models
on the live bot host. Three official sources supplied downloadable product
images that passed format and resolution checks. One more supplied a complete
specification sample, but its available images were below the pipeline's
quality threshold. A retailer supplied a matching product and a valid image,
while rate-limiting its specification page; that source remains unverified for
automatic data collection.

The update repairs the official Orient page route and reuses the existing
Shopify reference search for a retailer whose product URLs omit model numbers.
Exact-reference checks, image-quality limits, source priorities and spreadsheet
approval remain in place. Ten source domains have been sampled across the
first two batches; the remaining domains still require live verification.
