# Improved product data discovery from a configured retailer

A configured Michael Kors retailer now uses its public product listing and
pagination to find exact model pages when its sitemap does not provide a
usable product index. This helps the existing review workbook receive source
specifications for available models. The source is rate limited and bounded.

Its sampled images did not pass quality checks, so this update does not claim
image completion or end-to-end publication. The established human review and
publishing workflow remains in place.
