# Catalogue review and management reports

A reviewer can correct multiple independently collected watch references in one workbook. Returning the workbook preserves each product's identity. A workbook submitted by staff keeps its own review. If a delivery is interrupted, staff can reconcile the original file before requesting another copy.

Supplier questions update the existing review card as decisions change. Sales and operations reports, staff activity, product photography and content reports each reach the corresponding management topic. Tests exercised a 142-row synthetic workbook, a restart, access to delivery recovery, and changes to the same supplier card.
