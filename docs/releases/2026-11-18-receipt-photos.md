# Photos on counter receipts and a dedicated storefront topic

Counter receipts group the buyer, payment method, purchased items and final amount. A verified product photo appears on the receipt itself. Converting store images into JPEG before uploading them allows Telegram to display photos served by the store in WebP. An existing text receipt can acquire a photograph without moving to a new message.

During the transition between sales groups, new storefront orders can also appear in a dedicated topic. Album copies preserve the original images and new payment captions update their own copy. The original order group continues receiving its cards.

Testing covered text-to-photo edits, caption limits, repeat delivery, an interrupted first copy and rejection of an incorrect topic. Production verification included attaching a matched photograph to a previously published counter receipt.
