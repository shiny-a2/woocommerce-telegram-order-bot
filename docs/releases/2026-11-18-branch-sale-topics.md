# Branch sale receipts and company discussions

Each branch has a dedicated receipt topic. The management bot posts imported counter sales there and updates the original receipt when the next import corrects the payment method. A confirmed catalogue match can attach the watch photo. Receipts identify the branch, invoice, items, payment methods, discount and total.

Company orders, list reviews, financial documents, catalogue approvals and management reports have explicit destinations. Scheduled price reports are private outbound notices for designated administrators. Telegram requires topic-management permission for automatic topic creation.

The service was installed and its two topic bindings and authenticated store connections were checked. Synthetic delivery tests covered retries, repeat imports, changed payments, photo attachments and destination isolation. The first live customer receipt depends on a normal branch import.
